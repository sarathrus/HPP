About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2022, Hocus Pocus Pictures was created from our frustration at seeing so many stories already based on existing material. We love the anticipation of seeing a film or television show that has never been seen before. We believe that female empowered stories aren't restricted to only seeing more women on our screens; we wanted to support women behind the scenes. We want to promote and help fresh local talent in all areas of our industry. Run by Gold Coast locals, we are passionate about bringing this unique area to the forefront of storytelling.

Our Story Video

Our Mission

To empower women in the field of film and television to expand their talents, as well as to create and produce high-quality original films and television shows that support Australian creatives.

Our Vision

To be the leading independent production company creating original stories by filmmakers on the Gold Coast and Australia.
