About Us

Our Story

HPP Productions is a visionary film production company dedicated to creating compelling and original stories that captivate audiences worldwide. Founded by a team of passionate filmmakers, we bring together diverse talents and perspectives to craft unique cinematic experiences.

Our Mission

To create compelling and original stories that inspire, entertain, and connect with audiences worldwide.

Meet Our Team

Sarah Woodward

CEO, Founder, Filmmaker & Actor

Discovering the magic of cinema and storytelling at age 6, Sarah has cultivated a career of over 30 years experience in acting and writing. Recently, she's honed these skills and further expanded her knowledge of producing and directing by studying a BA Film and Television at Bond University. Her past acting training has seen her work with coaches Tony Knight in Australia and Anthony Meindl in Los Angeles. Sarah's first screenplay, 'Hidden Melodies' made the quarter finals of the Final Draft's Big Break Scriptwriting Competition and top 50 in the Historical Genre. Also holding a Master of Criminology degree from Bond, Sarah is determined to bring dynamic stories created by diverse Australian talent on our screens.

Marina Cerra

Co-Producer, Head of Casting & Writer

Marina has honed her experience as a co-producer and casting director though her work with other student films. She enjoys the planning and organisation aspect that goes into both these roles, and particular, loves to see and support new talent both in front of and behind the camera. Marina has interned with Jaggi Entertainment in their costume department and has aspirations to become a writer and casting agent.

Sarah Athalla Rusdi

Head of Social Media

Sarah is a creative and results-driven member with a passion for big and innovative ideas. With extensive experience in social media management and digital content creation, she excels at crafting engaging, high-impact campaigns that drive brand growth and audience engagement for Hocus Pocus Pictures. Always eager to test new limits, she aims to bring bold concepts to life and elevate HPP's presence across multiple platforms.

Lauren Bond

Head of Art Department

Lauren brings her creative vision and artistic expertise to every project. With a background in visual arts and set design, she ensures that each production maintains a high standard of visual excellence. Her attention to detail and innovative approach to art direction helps bring our stories to life with stunning visual elements.

Beck Bennett

Associate Writer

Rebecca loves the visually creative aspect of production design and location scout. She enjoys planning and collecting the props and costumes needed for each scene and ensuring the cast were camera ready. Rebecca has aspirations of working as a screenwriter and production designer, with a passion for seeing her stories and visions turned into magnificent film productions.
