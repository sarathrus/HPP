The Visitor

Fantasy | 2022

About the Short

In this intimate short film, a reclusive writer's life is turned upside down when a mysterious visitor arrives at their doorstep. What begins as an unwelcome interruption becomes a journey of self-discovery and unexpected connection.

Film Details

  • Director: Emily Chen
  • Writer: David Wilson
  • Producer: HPP Productions
  • Starring: Rachel Adams, James Miller
  • Runtime: 12 minutes
